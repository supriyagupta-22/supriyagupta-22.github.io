Auto Redaction System

Solo project · PII redaction for Indian documents · Jan 2026 – present

A tool that runs on your own machine and finds and redacts personal data in Indian documents.

  • Built a local tool that redacts 10 PII types (Aadhaar, PAN, card, passport, email, phone, names, etc.) from text, PDF, Word and scans.
  • Validated Aadhaar (Verhoeff) and card (Luhn) checksums; trained a spaCy NER model for names and locations on Faker data.
  • F1 of 1.00 on a 240-document synthetic split; fixed gaps over three rounds of adversarial testing. The synthetic split is easy, so the adversarial rounds are the more honest signal.
  • Added fixed masking and HMAC-SHA256 pseudonym modes, a SQLite audit log that stores only hashes, and CSV audit export.
